# August 2026 investor webinar — standalone pack

This folder is **not part of the cashier portal**. It is a self-contained briefing for a Wednesday 19 August 2026 webinar:

**Don’t let August cash leave your portfolio.**

Encourage holders of maturing Kenyan government paper and August coupons to reinvest in a CMA-licensed **Money Market Fund** or **Fixed Income Fund**.

## Files

| File | Use |
|---|---|
| `index.html` | Present this. Open in Chrome, press **F** for fullscreen, **← →** to move. |
| `August_2026_Reinvesting_Bond_Proceeds.pptx` | Same deck for Zoom/Teams screen share or to drop into Google Slides. |
| `SPEAKER_NOTES.md` | Timed script, likely Q&A, and sources for every number. |
| `assets/` | Compounding charts used in the PowerPoint. |
| `generate_pptx.py` | Regenerates the `.pptx` if you change numbers. |

## How to run the webinar

1. Put your firm name and logo on slides 1, 10 and 16 (HTML and PPTX).
2. Name the specific funds you are licensed to offer — the deck is deliberately generic.
3. Open `index.html` or the PowerPoint.
4. Keep `SPEAKER_NOTES.md` on a second screen.

## What the deck covers

1. August redemption calendar (FXD1/2016/010, IFB1/2020/011, T-bills, 26 Aug switch).
2. Why spending the principal kills compounding — chart of KES 1,000,000 over 15 years.
3. Case for Money Market vs Fixed Income, and a 60-second choice test.
4. This week’s action list (17 / 24 / 26 August).

Figures are industry snapshots as of mid-August 2026 (CBK, KNBS, SIB weekly, published MMF/FIF tables). They are not a forecast and not a recommendation of any named unit trust.
