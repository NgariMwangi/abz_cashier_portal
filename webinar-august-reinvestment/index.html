Why reinvest instead of spend

Maturity is not a bonus

The principal was already yours. Spending it shrinks the machine that was paying you 10–15% coupons.

Coupons are easy to hide

A few hundred thousand shillings of interest does not feel like a decision. Repeated twice a year, it is the whole return.

Cash is taxed by inflation

6.5% inflation versus a 2% account is a real loss. A ~9% MMF still compounds in real terms.

You cannot recapture skipped years

The steep part of the compounding curve is later. Ten idle years cannot be “caught up” with one good year.

Liquidity is already built in

You do not need to spend the bond to stay liquid. An MMF is typically T+2 to T+4, often from KES 1,000.

Replace the expired contract

FXD1/2016/010 paid 15.039%. That contract ends on 17 August. A fund puts you back into a book of bills and bonds the next day.

Two CMA-licensed homes for the proceeds

Money Market Fund

Park it. Stay liquid. Beat the current account.

  • Holds T-bills, short bonds, call and fixed deposits
  • Typical industry yield ~9% gross / ~7.6% net (H1 2026 average)
  • Interest calculated daily, paid after 15% WHT
  • Access usually within 2–4 working days
  • Best when the money may be needed inside 12 months
  • KES 442bn of industry MMF AUM as of March 2026

Fixed Income Fund

Put duration back to work. Replace the expired bond.

  • Holds a ladder of Treasury and quality corporate bonds
  • Category average ~11.9% gross / ~10.1% net (June 2026)
  • More yield than an MMF — and more price movement
  • Sensible holding period: 12 months and beyond
  • ~250 bps extra versus the MMF average in mid-2026
  • A closer substitute for the bond that just matured

The case for the Money Market Fund

Use it when the August cash is a bridge — school fees in January, a property completion, or “I have not decided yet”.

Beats idle cash immediately

91-day T-bills are ~8.8%. A decent MMF often matches or beats that, with daily compounding and no auction paperwork.

No need to pick a bond

You do not have to bid the 24 August switch or the next IFB. The manager already holds a book of bills and short paper.

You stay in control

Units can usually be withdrawn in a few days. The money is working, but it is not locked.

A disciplined default

Standing instruction: every DhowCSD credit → MMF. Spending then requires a conscious withdrawal, not an accident.

Professional cash management

Custodian + trustee + CMA licence. This is not a Sacco promise or a WhatsApp “investment”.

USD option exists

If the proceeds are dollar-linked, dollar MMFs returned about 4.3% net in H1 2026.

The case for the Fixed Income Fund

Use it when the August cash was long-term capital — the 10-year bond that just matured should not become a 91-day afterthought.

Replace the expired coupon engine

FXD1/2016/010 paid 15%. New 10-year FXD paper is around 12.8%. A bond fund keeps you in that market without timing one auction.

A ladder, not a single name

One matured issue becomes a diversified book — several tenors, reopenings, and (in some funds) quality corporates.

The extra 2–3 points compound

On KES 1m, 250 bps extra versus an MMF is about KES 25,000 a year at the start — and more as the base grows.

Yields are still useful

CBR is 8.75%. Long bonds still clear ~12–14%. Locking duration now is how you keep income if policy rates drift lower.

Tax-aware IFB exposure

Infrastructure bonds pay coupons with no WHT. Funds that hold IFBs pass through some of that tax advantage.

Stay invested through the switch

You do not have to decide by 24 August whether to switch FXD1/2012/015. The fund can hold duration for you.
